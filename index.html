Innovation & Efficiency

All our innovation aims to lower the cost per km of transportation to benefit shippers while supporting transporters.

Create Pickup Request

Click the button to request pickup by selecting location, destination, and preferred truck.

2
Order Matching

Headsup logistics matches your cargo to one of our vetted transport partners. You receive details on truck and driver.

3
Tracking Code Sent

A tracking code is sent to you via your preferred channel, either WhatsApp or email, to monitor your shipment.

4
In Transit

Your cargo is picked up and transported to its destination. You receive live status updates during transit.

5
Delivered & Confirmed

Delivery is confirmed electronically by the recipient. Headsup logisitcs verifies documentation to ensure all is in order, then releases payment to the transporter.
